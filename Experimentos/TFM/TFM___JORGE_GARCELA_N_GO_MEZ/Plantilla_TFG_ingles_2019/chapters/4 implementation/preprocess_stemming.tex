Typically, stemming is performed when using means of natural language processing. This is because words sharing the same stem may have the same meaning linguistically and would reduce the number of words in the vocabulary. However, since the majority of our corpus consists of gender-related terms in Spanish, applying stemming is not recommendable in this context, as it could remove or alter gender-specific markers in the words, potentially leading to the loss of crucial information related to gender.
