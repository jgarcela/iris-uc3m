A preliminary analysis was performed for the languages of the tweets in our dataset. As the majority of our corpus is in Spanish, representing 63.14\% of the total tweets, we decided to discard other languages to facilitate the classification. As seen in Figure~\ref{fig:languages}, since there were not many tweets for some classes, a manual review was made and it was checked that tweets that belong to \textit{'cy', 'ht', 'in', 'lt', 'qam', 'tl'} and \textit{'und'} were mainly tweets in Spanish that had been misclassified at the origin. For this reason, they have been considered also for the experiments.
