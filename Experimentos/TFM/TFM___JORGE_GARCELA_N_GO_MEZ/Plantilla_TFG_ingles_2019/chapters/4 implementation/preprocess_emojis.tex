Since the proliferation of text messaging, emojis have been part of our day-to-day basis in online communication. Not only do they act as non-verbal indicators of emotions but also as a way to add context to our texts. For that reason, it is important to maintain the meaning of such emojis in the tweets. The \textit{"emoji"} library of Python let us do that task by converting the emoji to text.
