When dealing with text, we need to be careful as two words may be the same in natural language but when represented in text, the way we write them matters. For humans, the words \textit{"sports", "SPORTS" or "Sports"} are basically the same word. Even though they are written differently, their meaning is the same. However, computers, especially algorithms, treat text in a case-sensitive way. For that reason, lower casing is a common approach that is followed when applying natural language processing techniques. Therefore, for the purpose of this thesis, lower casing has been applied to the complete dataset of tweets.
