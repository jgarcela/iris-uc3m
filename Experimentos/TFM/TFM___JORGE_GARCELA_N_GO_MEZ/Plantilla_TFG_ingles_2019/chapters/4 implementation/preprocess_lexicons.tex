As described in Section~\ref{sec:hs_detection}, studies such as \cite{plazaDelArco-2020} have tackled text-based HS detection with lexicon-based approaches. In the study, they propose four different Spanish lexicons: Inmigrant, Misogyny, Xenophobia and Insults. Because the focus of this work is HS towards women, the lexicons that were considered are misogyny and insults. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Insults Lexicon:} featuring an insults list from \cite{plazaDelArco-2020}.
    \item \textbf{Misogyny Lexicon:} consisting in a compilation of misogyny words from \cite{plazaDelArco-2020}.
\end{itemize}
