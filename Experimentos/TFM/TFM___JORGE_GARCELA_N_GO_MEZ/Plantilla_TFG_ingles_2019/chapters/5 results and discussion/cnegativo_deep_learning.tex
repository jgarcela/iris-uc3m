Following the approach defined in Section~\ref{implementation_dl}, we considered 15 total experiments for the Deep Learning approach in this task. However, the RoBERTuito model for "Full Context" ({\cnegfcrobertuito}) did not execute entirely due to exceeding its context window.

From Table~\ref{tab:cnegativo-dl-bestmodels_metrics} and Figure~\ref{fig:exp_cnegativo_dl_max_f1}, we can appreciate that the presence of context did not always lead to better performance across all classes. Specifically, the "Tweet Context" and "Full Context" settings showed a decrease in performance for several classes, with "Desprestigiar Deportista Autora" being the only class that maintained a relatively high F1-Score across all contexts, likely due to being the majority class. Moreover, for "Desprestigiar Víctima" we can see that we achieved the highest F1-Score in "Full Context" for model {\cnegfcroberta}, but for the other models, we obtained zero performance. Notably, the "Desprestigiar Acto" and "Insultos" classes saw an increase of F1-Score in "Tweet Context", but a decline when moving to "Full Context", suggesting that adding more contextual information, in this case, may have introduced noise or complexity to the models.

Similarly as in Section~\ref{sec:cnegativo_ml}, we have decided to choose \textbf{\cnegtcbeto} as the best DL model for "Contenido Negativo" because it scored the highest Weighted F1-Score 0.531, with also good performance for the classes with 0.417, 0.471, 0.375 and 0.676 for "Desprestigiar Víctima", "Desprestigiar Acto", "Insultos" and "Desprestigiar Deportista Autora", respectively. In particular, in Figure~\ref{fig:exp_cnegativo_dl_bestmodel} we can see in more detail the confusion matrix for this model. From this figure, we notice that the model behaved well in predicting "Desprestigiar Deportista Autora", with a high true positive rate of 0.767 (23 correct out of 30 total instances). However, we can also see that there was a tendency to confuse "Desprestigiar Acto" with "Desprestigiar Deportista Autora" and "Desprestigiar Víctima". Moreover, the misclassification between these two classes was somewhat expected and occurred in a reasonable manner, as the categories share similar meanings.
