%----------
%	DATA SOURCE
%----------	

The main dataset that has been used during the development of the thesis has been created and provided by Professor Alba Adá. Her research provides a detailed analysis of hate speech on X.com (formerly Twitter) about the tweets that were published by the players of the Women Spanish Football team \cite{lameiras-2024}. The corpus, which was manually labelled by Professor Alba Adá, contains the messages and responses to tweets that 17 out of 23 players published in \textit{X (formerly Twitter)}, on the 25th of August 2023 (5 days after the aggression to Jenni Hermoso), and the same day that a joint statement was released by herself and FUTPRO women players association. Since Elon Musk acquired X, accessibility for research and retrieving tweets has been a complicated task. This is due to the increase in subscription pricing, limitation on the number of tweets that can be retrieved, etc. Hence, the use of third-party tools was necessary. In this case, tweets were collected using \textit{"Twitter Replies Exporter"}\footnote{From \url{https://twrepliesexport.extensionsfox.com/}, last visited 04/07/24}, property of \textit{"ExtensionsFox"}, on 27th October 2023. This tool allowed the recovery of responses to any given tweet including metadata, such as the number of views or replies, and even information about the user like the biography and the number of followers. Moreover, the collection of tweets corresponds to replies to the original tweet and does not include secondary threads (responses to replies).
